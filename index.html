01

4 BASE CARDS PER PLAYER

Every player starts with 4 Number cards placed face-up in front of them. If an Action card is dealt during setup, reshuffle it into the Draw Pile immediately.

02

DRAW PILE & NUMBER DISCARD

Center draw deck. Whenever a player makes a wrong guess, that card goes to the Number Discard. When the Draw Pile runs dry, only the Number Discard is reshuffled.

03

ACTION DISCARD (PERMANENT)

Played Action cards are sent to the dedicated Action Discard pile. Action cards are never reshuffled back into the draw deck, making deck tracking essential as the endgame approaches.

Rulebook Condensed

HOW TURNS WORK

PREDICT & FLIP

On your turn, choose one of your face-up cards and call HIGHER, LOWER, or MATCH. Flip the top card of the Draw Pile.

  • Correct Guess: You stay alive and force any opponent to predict on a card of your choice.
  • Incorrect Guess: Your base card is discarded to the Number Discard pile.
  • Ties: Ties lose automatically unless MATCH was explicitly called.

ACTION CARDS (MAX 1 HELD)

When drawn during a flip, an Action card can be played immediately or held for a future turn (maximum of 1 in hand).

  • DOUBLE DOWN: Force an opponent to make 2 consecutive predictions on 1 card.
  • TRAP: Set a blind card face-down that an opponent must flip on their turn.
  • LOOP: Locks an opponent onto a single card until they miss a guess.
